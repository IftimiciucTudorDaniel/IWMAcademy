Clarity before commitment

Common questions, clear answers.

What exactly do I receive after joining?

Access to the research desk(s) included in your paid membership, structured trade-plan records, updates, educational materials and the appropriate private Discord channels, once implemented and enabled.

Will each trade show buy, sell and stop-loss levels?

The intended member record includes direction, entry, stop loss, targets, changes and closing information. Some plans may be cancelled or never triggered; those outcomes are recorded too. The sample prices on this demo page are entirely fictional.

Is Swing Trade available separately?

Yes, Swing Trade is included as a dedicated desk in this website redesign, with its own membership selection and example trade. Actual availability and billing must follow the approved product catalogue.

Can I subscribe monthly, for 6 months or for 1 year?

The design supports all three periods. The production checkout must display the exact term, price, whether the plan renews automatically and the next charge date before consent.

Can I stop my subscription renewing?

The production member dashboard should let you turn off future renewals without contacting support. It must show your paid-through date and explain any refund or access rules accurately.

Does IWM execute trades or manage my brokerage account?

This concept presents research, educational trade plans and a self-directed trade journal. It does not execute orders, hold funds or manage client accounts. Any additional regulated service would need separate review, implementation and approval.
